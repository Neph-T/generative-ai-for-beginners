Generative AI in Agriculture (Drones and Security Perimeter)
Problem
Farmers in South Africa face severe operational challenges, including delayed crop disease detection and rampant livestock theft across vast, isolated properties. Traditional crop scouting requires hours of manual labor, while standard perimeter fences leave massive blind spots. By the time a breach or stock loss is discovered hours later, perpetrators have escaped and entire yields are damaged, creating massive financial strain and safety risks for rural farming communities.

How I Would Use AI
- I will combine smart IoT perimeter sensors (vibration detectors and tripwires) with an autonomous, 
- AI-driven drone surveillance fleet powered by Microsoft Azure IoT Hub and Azure AI. When a perimeter sensor detects a break-in, a drone is automatically dispatched to the GPS coordinates. 00000
- Using edge computer vision and high-resolution thermal imaging, the AI analyzes the live feed in real time—distinguishing false alarms from real threats (such as human intruders or stolen cattle/sheep) and alerting the farmer instantly. 
- Additionally, scheduled daytime drone flights use multispectral visual models to monitor crop health and identify pest outbreaks before they spread.

Impact
- Doing what wasn't possible before: Replaces slow, dangerous human security patrols with instant, 
- autonomous visual verification within seconds of a perimeter breach, day or night.

Proactive Security: Shifts farm security from reactive investigation to real-time interception, 
- protecting lives and drastically cutting livestock loss.

Efficiency & Speed: Reduces manual crop inspection time by up to 90%, 
- enabling targeted interventions that save input costs and increase total harvest yield.
